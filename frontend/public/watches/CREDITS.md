# Watch photography: licence record

Editorial photographs used as each watch's lead image and on the homepage stage.
All are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the
[Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no
attribution required. They are illustrative and not always the exact piece
listed; the owner's own photograph of each watch is attached as its second image
(`/products/<slug>.png`). Replace these with the store's own photography when
available, keeping the watch centred in a 3:2 frame.

Processing: cropped 3:2 with the watch centred at a consistent scale (so the
homepage transition reads as one watch becoming the next), desaturated 20% and
toned 16% toward the brand's brown. Downloaded 2026-09-22.
